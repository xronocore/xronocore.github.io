Unstack

A cute tile-matching puzzle for iPhone

The tiles are piled on top of each other, and you can only tap the ones on top. Tap a tile to move it into your deck, match three of the same picture to clear them, and clear the whole board before the deck fills up.

What's in the game

  • 12 adorable sea creatures: crabs, whales, jellyfish, turtles and more
  • Every level can be won, and levels keep getting trickier
  • Four difficulty levels, from relaxing Easy to brain-bending Hell
  • Undo, Move out and Shuffle for when you're stuck
  • Daily rewards and coins to collect
  • Free to play. No account needed.
